# tiwhub Mascot Concepts

Four original mascot directions for the Oasis Pages identity. The files are editable SVG concepts, drawn in the shared Oasis blue, navy, cyan, and blue-mist palette.

| Concept | Working name | Character | Brand role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | น้องบุ๊กกี้ (Booky) | A friendly open book with a cyan bookmark | Most directly tied to the existing tiwhub mark; approachable learning companion |
| 02 | น้องฮับบี้ (Hubby) | A small learning-hub robot with a book on its chest | Modern, helpful guide for parents and school teams |
| 03 | น้องปีกฟ้า (Pikfa) | A curious blue owl carrying an open book | Warm, observant, and well suited to learning tips and progress updates |
| 04 | น้องประกาย (Prakai) | A bright learning spark holding a little book | Energetic, celebratory companion for milestones and encouragement |

These are concept explorations, not yet a selected production mascot. Names are working names. After selection, refine the chosen character into a compact avatar, expression set, and simplified small-size mark before using it throughout the product.
